# 2048 Mundial

A fan-made World Cup edition of the 2048 sliding-tile puzzle. Eleven national shirts, from Cameroon at 2 to Italy at 2048.

Una edición del Mundial: once camisetas de selecciones, de Camerún en el 2 a Italia en el 2048.

**Play:** [English](https://tile2048.github.io/2048-MUNDIAL/) · [Español](https://tile2048.github.io/2048-MUNDIAL/es/)

## How to play

Arrow keys move the whole board; a swipe does the same on a phone. Two tiles of the same value merge into the next one on the ladder, and the value of the new tile is added to your score. The run ends when the board is full and no two neighbours match, so leave a corner free and plan one merge ahead.
