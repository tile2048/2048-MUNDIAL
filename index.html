How to play: Use your arrow keys to move the tiles. When two shirts of the same team touch, they advance a round!
Hit 'Z' to take back one or more moves.

2048 Mundial puts eleven World Cup shirts on a 4×4 board, arranged as a ladder rather than a league table. Cameroon starts it at 2, then Costa Rica, England, Uruguay, the Netherlands, Nigeria, Germany, Argentina, Spain and Brazil, with Italy closing the run at 2048. Arrow keys or a swipe move every shirt at once; two of the same nation knit into the next shirt up and add points. Z takes back the last move, which helps when only one square is free. The game ends when no two neighbours can be joined, so leave a column open and plan two rounds ahead. Carry a shirt to Italy to win the cup.
